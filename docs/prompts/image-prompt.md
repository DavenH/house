On approach, the impression is of a rediscovered castle in the midst of a forest.

The placement of gables, chimneys, spires, and hip rooves occur at a harmonious pacing. 

Its outline evokes a city skyline, rising and falling without harsh break. 

The appearance of century-defying solidity, while letting in far more light than a dingy castle. 

Each area has a feeling of generous space, though the dining and kitchen rooms offer a enclosed cozy intimacy. 

The kitchen looks out at the islands in the distance, and the house sits on a knoll surrounded by hardwood trees, itself halfway up a ridge.
